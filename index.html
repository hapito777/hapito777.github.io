    Common questions

    Is a self‑hosted model as good as ChatGPT or Claude?

    The best open models are now within a few months of the leading closed models, and for most company tasks — writing, summarising, search over documents, coding help — staff notice little difference. In the pilot you compare them on your own work before buying anything.

    What does it cost?

    Hardware ranges from under $15,000 for a team of 20–50 people to several hundred thousand dollars for frontier models serving a large organisation. The calculator above gives an indicative figure; the assessment gives you a firm one.

    Does any of our data leave our network?

    No. The model, the search index and the chat interface all run on your hardware. We can deploy fully offline if required.

    Can it use our existing documents and logins?

    Yes. We connect file shares, SharePoint, Confluence and databases, and staff sign in with your existing accounts. Answers only draw on documents each person is already allowed to see.

    Are Chinese open models safe to use?

    When self‑hosted, the model sends nothing anywhere — it is a file of numbers running on your server. The real questions are licence terms and how the model behaves, which we review with you. Western alternatives such as gpt‑oss, Gemma and Mistral are always on the table.

    Who maintains it after go‑live?

    Your IT team, with our documentation and training — or us, through the Managed AI subscription.
