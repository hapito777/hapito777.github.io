    What I do for you

    Each engagement is scoped to your company. You can take the whole path or just the part you need.

    Infrastructure assessment

    Review of your servers, network, power and cooling, identity provider and data sources, so the AI fits what you already run.

    Model selection

    Shortlist and benchmark open models on your real tasks and languages, including a licence check for commercial use.

    Hardware sizing & procurement

    A written sizing report, vendor quotes compared line by line, and a setup that leaves room to grow.

    Deployment

    vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp or Ollama on Docker or Kubernetes, with an OpenAI‑compatible API and single sign‑on chat interface.

    Company knowledge (RAG)

    Connect file shares, SharePoint, Confluence and databases so answers cite your documents, with existing access rights respected.

    Security & operations

    Network isolation, audit logging, GPU monitoring, model updates and a support agreement with agreed response times.
